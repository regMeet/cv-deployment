# "Tell me more..." — going deep on follow-ups

> Many candidates fall apart here. You need to dig into **decisions, trade-offs, and impact**.

## Strong answer (template)

I chose that approach because it provided a good **balance between performance and simplicity**.

There was an alternative that could have been more optimal from a performance standpoint, but it would have introduced **additional operational complexity** and made the system harder to maintain.

Given the current requirements and expected scale, I prioritized a solution that was **easier to evolve and faster to deliver**.

We can always iterate later if the constraints change.

## What to dig into when asked for more detail

- **Decisions you made** — and why.
- **Trade-offs you considered** — what you gave up.
- **Real impact** — what changed afterwards.

## Closing mindsets to weave into multiple answers

- **Ownership**: "I took responsibility for…"
- **Urgency**: "I prioritized restoring service…"
- **Communication**: "I kept stakeholders informed…"
- **Quality**: "I added tests / monitoring…"
- **Impact**: "It reduced X%, improved Y…"
