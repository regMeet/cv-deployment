# Strategic / business thinking

> This is what separates an average senior from a staff candidate.

## Strong answer

I always try to understand **how my work impacts the business**, not just from a technical perspective but in terms of outcomes.

When making decisions, I consider factors like **scalability, cost, and time to market**, and I align my approach with the product's priorities.

For example, I might choose a **simpler solution to deliver value faster**, or **invest more in scalability** if the feature is expected to grow.

I also try to **anticipate future needs** and **avoid overengineering**, balancing short-term delivery with long-term sustainability.

Overall, my goal is to make technical decisions that **support business goals**, not just technical excellence.

## Tip

Mention concrete dimensions: scalability, cost, time-to-market. Avoid abstract platitudes like "I align with the business".
