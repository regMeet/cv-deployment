# How do you report progress?

> Senior = nobody has to chase you for updates.

## Strong answer

I make sure progress is always visible and easy to track.

I keep tickets updated with **meaningful information** — not just status, but context, decisions, and next steps.

I actively participate in dailies, highlighting progress, risks, and any blockers early.

If something is critical, I **proactively communicate updates to stakeholders** without waiting to be asked, focusing on impact and timelines rather than just activity.

Overall, I aim to **reduce uncertainty** so the team and stakeholders always know where things stand.

## What sounds senior

- Pushing context, not pulling it from you.
- Communicating in terms of **impact and timelines**, not "I worked on X today".
- Surfacing blockers immediately instead of waiting for the next standup.
