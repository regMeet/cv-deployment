# How do you approach a problem?

> Interviewers want to see **method, not improvisation**. Bonus points for metrics and trade-offs.

## Strong answer

First, I make sure I fully understand the problem before jumping into solutions.
I break it down into smaller parts, validate assumptions, and prioritize based on impact.

Then I define **clear success metrics** — such as latency, error rate, or business KPIs — so I can measure whether the solution is actually effective.

From there, I evaluate **trade-offs**, like speed vs quality or simplicity vs scalability, depending on the context.

I implement iteratively and continuously validate with data and team feedback.

## Tips to level up

- Mention metrics (latency, error rate, throughput, business KPIs).
- Talk about trade-offs explicitly (speed vs quality, simplicity vs scalability).
- Show iteration + validation, not waterfall.
