# How do you mentor or grow more junior engineers?

> Senior+ is expected to **multiply the team**, not just ship code.

## Strong answer

I focus on helping juniors build **judgment**, not just deliver tickets.

In code reviews and pairing, I try to explain **the why** behind decisions — trade-offs, constraints, alternatives — so they can apply that thinking on their own next time.

I also create space for **safe experimentation**: giving them ownership of a meaningful piece, being available when they get stuck, but not jumping in to solve it for them.

I encourage them to **ask better questions** and **document their thinking**, which usually accelerates their growth more than any specific technique.

For longer-term development, I look at where they want to grow and try to **align their work with stretch opportunities** — not just easy or hard, but genuinely growth-inducing.

## Signals

- Teaching judgment, not just answers.
- Ownership > supervision.
- Investing in their growth direction, not just current tasks.
