# How do you think about automation?

> Key signal for senior: you scale yourself and the team through automation.

## Strong answer

I see automation as a **key enabler for both quality and scalability**.

Whenever a process is repetitive, error-prone, or critical, I try to automate it.

This includes **CI/CD pipelines, automated testing at different levels, and validations** to catch issues early.

I also focus on automating **operational tasks** — like monitoring, alerts, and deployment workflows — to reduce manual intervention and improve reliability.

Overall, the goal is to increase delivery speed while maintaining consistency and reducing human error.

## Tip

Frame it as **velocity + quality + reliability**, not just "less work for me".
