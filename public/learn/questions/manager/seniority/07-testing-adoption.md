# Team is reluctant to testing / QA / TDD — what do you do?

> They're evaluating **influence**, not technical knowledge. Be pragmatic, not dogmatic.

## Strong answer

I try to introduce testing and quality practices **incrementally**, focusing on **demonstrating value rather than enforcing rules**.

I usually start with **high-impact areas** — like critical paths or parts of the system that have caused production issues — and add tests there.

When the team sees that this **reduces bugs and improves confidence in deployments**, adoption tends to grow naturally.

I also keep things pragmatic, choosing the right level of testing depending on the context, instead of pushing for strict TDD everywhere.

Over time, I aim to build a **culture where quality is part of the development process**, not a separate phase.

## Key idea

Pragmatic > dogmatic. You influence by showing wins, not by mandating practices.
