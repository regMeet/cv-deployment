# How do you handle something urgent?

> They want to see calm + prioritization + communication, not heroics.

## Strong answer

In urgent situations, my priority is to **restore service as quickly as possible**, even if that means applying a temporary workaround.

I stay calm and focus on minimizing impact, typically by identifying the **fastest safe mitigation** — like a rollback, feature flag, or isolating the failing component.

At the same time, I **communicate clearly and proactively** with stakeholders about status, impact, and expected timelines.

Once the situation is stable, I perform a **root cause analysis** and implement a proper long-term fix.

Finally, I add preventive measures such as monitoring, alerts, and tests to avoid recurrence.

## Mindset to convey

- **Mitigate first, fix later** — restore service before debugging deeply.
- **Comms in parallel** — don't go silent.
- **Postmortem + prevention** — close the loop, don't move on too fast.
