# Why do you want to work here?

> Don't say "money".

## Strong answer

> "I'm interested in the **scale and technical challenges** you're solving. I also value teams that prioritize **engineering excellence and ownership**."

## How to tailor

- Mention something specific about the company (product, scale, tech, mission).
- Connect it to **your strengths** and what you want to grow in.
- Show alignment with engineering culture (autonomy, quality, ownership).

## Avoid

- Generic answers that fit any company.
- Pure compensation focus.
- Negative comments about your current job.
