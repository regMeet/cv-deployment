# Tell me about a time you were under pressure

> They want to see **calm prioritization**, not heroics.

## Strong example

> "During a production outage, I stayed calm, **prioritized restoring service first**, then root cause analysis. We implemented monitoring and alerting improvements to prevent recurrence."

## Mindset

- Mitigate before debug.
- Communicate up and across, even mid-fire.
- Postmortem + prevention close the loop.
