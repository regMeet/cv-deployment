# How do you deal with ambiguity?

> Critical for senior roles. They want to see you **make progress without perfect clarity**.

## Strong answer

> "I break ambiguity into **smaller assumptions**. I clarify requirements with stakeholders, define milestones, and iterate quickly. I prefer **delivering small increments** instead of waiting for perfect clarity."

## What signals seniority

- You don't freeze without a spec.
- You **surface assumptions** explicitly so they can be validated.
- You ship in increments and adjust based on feedback.
- You ask the right questions of the right people.
