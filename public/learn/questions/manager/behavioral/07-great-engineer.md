# What makes a great engineer?

> Position yourself. Your answer is also a self-portrait.

## Strong answer

> "Strong **problem-solving, ownership, and communication**. Not just writing code, but understanding **trade-offs, scalability, and business impact**."

## Pillars to mention

- **Problem-solving** — beyond just coding.
- **Ownership** — sees something broken, fixes it.
- **Communication** — clear, proactive, reduces uncertainty.
- **Trade-offs** — knows when to optimize and when not to.
- **Business impact** — connects tech work to outcomes.

## Tip

Pick 3–4 traits. Saying "all of them" sounds shallow.
