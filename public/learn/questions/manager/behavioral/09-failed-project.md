# Tell me about a project that failed

> They want **honesty + learning**.

## Strong example

> "We **over-engineered a system early without validating requirements**. It delayed delivery. Since then, I prioritize **iterative development and early feedback**."

## Recipe

1. Real failure (not a near-miss disguised as a failure).
2. What went wrong — be specific about the cause.
3. What you changed in your approach as a result.
4. Evidence the change worked.

## Avoid

- Blaming external factors only.
- "Nothing really failed."
- A failure with no clear lesson learned.
