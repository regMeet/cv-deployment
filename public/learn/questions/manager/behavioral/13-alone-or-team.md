# Alone or in a team? (trap question)

> Common trap. Don't pick one.

## Strong answer

> "**Both.** I can work independently, but I believe the **best results come from collaboration and feedback**."

## Why it's a trap

- "Alone" sounds like you don't collaborate.
- "Team" sounds like you can't ship without supervision.
- The right answer is **comfortable in both modes, with judgment to know when each fits**.

## Optional follow-up

If pushed, give an example of solo deep work AND collaborative work, both yielding good outcomes.
