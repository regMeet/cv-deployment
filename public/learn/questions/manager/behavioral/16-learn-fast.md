# Tell me about a time you had to learn something new fast

> They're testing **adaptability** + how you bootstrap in unfamiliar territory.

## Strong example

> "I was assigned to a project that required deep work in a part of the stack I hadn't touched before. I started by **mapping the system** end-to-end: reading the existing code, talking to the previous owner, and identifying the **critical paths**. Within the first few days I focused on a small, low-risk change to validate my mental model. Once that worked, I scaled up to bigger changes. Within a couple of weeks I was leading the workstream and onboarding others into the same area."

## Recipe

1. **Map before deep-diving** — understand the lay of the land first.
2. **Talk to people** — current/former owners save you weeks.
3. **Validate with a small change** before committing to bigger ones.
4. **Document as you go** — your notes become onboarding material for the next person.

## Signals

- Comfortable in ambiguity.
- Pragmatic — learn enough to ship, deep-dive later if needed.
- Use people, code, and docs in parallel — not just one source.
