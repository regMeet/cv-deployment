# What are your strengths and weaknesses?

> Realistic balance. Not a fake-humble flex, not a confession.

## Strong answer

> "**Strength**: I'm very strong in **backend architecture and debugging complex systems**.
>
> **Weakness**: I used to **go too deep into optimization early**, but now I focus more on delivering value first."

## Recipe for the weakness

1. Real weakness (not a stealth strength like "I'm a perfectionist").
2. What it caused.
3. How you've actively worked on it.
4. Evidence of progress.

## Recipe for the strength

- Specific (not "I'm a team player").
- Backed by an example you can talk about if pushed.
