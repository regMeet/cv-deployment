# Tell me about a mistake you made

> They want **maturity + learning**, NOT perfection.

## Strong example

> "I once underestimated the impact of a schema change in a shared database. It caused downstream services to fail. I rolled back quickly, added versioning to our schema, and introduced contract testing. Since then, we haven't had similar issues."

## Avoid

- "I haven't really made mistakes."
- Blaming others.
- Trivial mistakes (typos) — they want a real one.

## Recipe

1. **Real mistake** with real impact.
2. **Quick recovery** — what you did immediately.
3. **Systemic fix** — how you prevented recurrence.
4. **Outcome** — no repeat issues.
