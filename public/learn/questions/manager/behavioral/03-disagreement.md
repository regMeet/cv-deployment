# How do you handle disagreements with teammates?

> They want **collaboration without ego**.

## Strong example

> "I focus on **data over opinions**. In a disagreement about architecture, I proposed we prototype both approaches and compare performance and complexity. We chose the simpler solution based on evidence."

## Key idea

The goal isn't to **win the argument** — it's to **solve the problem**. Show you can disagree and commit.

## Mindset to convey

- Lead with curiosity, not defense.
- Use data/prototypes to settle technical disagreements.
- Once a decision is made, commit fully — even if it wasn't your choice.
