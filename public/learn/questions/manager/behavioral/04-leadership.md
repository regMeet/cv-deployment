# Tell me about a time you showed leadership

> Even if you're not a manager, they expect **ownership and initiative**.

## Strong example

> "Our deployment process was manual and error-prone. I took initiative to introduce CI/CD pipelines, **reducing deployment time from 30 minutes to under 5** and eliminating human errors."

## What "leadership" looks like at IC level

- Spotting a systemic issue and fixing it without being asked.
- Driving a decision when the team is stuck.
- Mentoring or unblocking others.
- Owning a cross-team initiative end-to-end.

## Tip

Lead with the **gap you saw**, then the **action you took**, then the **measurable outcome**.
