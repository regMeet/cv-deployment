# How do you handle feedback?

> They want **growth mindset**.

## Strong answer

> "I see feedback as a **tool for improvement**. I actively **ask for it** and try to **apply it quickly**."

## What sounds senior

- Asking for feedback proactively, not waiting for review cycles.
- Distinguishing between feedback you'll act on now vs later.
- Following up — "I tried what you suggested, here's how it went."
- Giving feedback with the same care you ask for it.
