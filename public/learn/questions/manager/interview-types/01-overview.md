# Types of interview questions (reference)

> Most senior+ interviews are a mix of **Behavioral + Competency-based + System/Product thinking**. Knowing which type you're in helps you frame the answer right.

## 1. Behavioral

The most standard label. Questions about **past experiences and behavior**.

- "Tell me about a challenge…"
- "How do you handle pressure?"

> Premise: past behavior predicts future performance.

## 2. Situational

Same shape, but **hypothetical**.

- "What would you do if…?"
- "How would you handle X situation?"

## 3. Cultural Fit / Cultural Add

How you fit (or **add**) to the team.

- Values.
- Way of working.
- Communication style.

> Many companies now use "cultural add" (what you bring) instead of just "fit".

## 4. Competency-Based

More formal / corporate. Evaluates specific competencies:

- Leadership.
- Ownership.
- Communication.
- Decision-making.

> "Tell me about a time you showed leadership."

## 5. System Thinking / Product Thinking (senior+)

How you think as a senior/staff engineer.

- "How do you ensure accountability?"
- "How do you handle ambiguity?"
- "How do you think about trade-offs?"

> No longer just behavior — they're testing **how you reason**.

## TL;DR

For senior/staff roles, expect a blend:

> **Behavioral + Competency-based + System / Product thinking**
